When crimes occur at multiple locations across dates, parsing tower dumps manually takes weeks. Our engine performs exact set intersection across spatio-temporal geofences in milliseconds:

Digital Evidence Integrity

⛓️ Blockchain Evidence Locker & BSA 2023 / Sec 65B Compliance

Cryptographic non-repudiation, tamper-evident audit trails, and automated judicial dossier export.

SHA-256 Ingestion Proof

Every raw CDR file, audio intercept, or FIR PDF is hashed at the exact second of ingestion. The hash is committed to a permissioned blockchain ledger, guaranteeing defense lawyers cannot claim police tampering.

Investigative Chain-of-Custody

Every action taken by an officer—filtering a call list, tagging a suspect, or exporting a graph—is cryptographically signed with their Police Officer ID (PKI certificate) on the ledger.

Automated BSA 2023 Certificate

Generates court-ready electronic evidence certificates adhering strictly to the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 and Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act with verifiable QR codes.
